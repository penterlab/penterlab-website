Mischa Selig and Angelos Galanopoulos showed their works at EHA 2026.

Mischa [showed](https://penterlab.github.io/penterlab-website-PDF/PF657.pdf) his work on large-scale multiparameter whole-slide imaging to uncover spatial niches along the MDS/AML continuum. 

Angelos [showcased](https://penterlab.github.io/penterlab-website-PDF/PF449.pdf) our improved nanoranger v2 workflow by dissecting transcriptomic features of SRSF2mut AML. 

Congratulations to Angelos for winning an EHA travel award! 
Congratulations to Mischa for receiving an EHA mentorship scholarship!
