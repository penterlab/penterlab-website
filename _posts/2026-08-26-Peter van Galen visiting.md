We had a great day with [Peter van Galen](https://www.hsci.harvard.edu/people/peter-van-galen-phd) who visited the lab and gave a wonderful overview on 
his impressive work at the [CompCancer](https://www.comp-cancer.de/) Colloquium.

Dr. Peter van Galen is an Associate Professor at Harvard Medical School and Brigham and Women's Hospital, where he directs a research laboratory studying the human blood system. His research combines single-cell genomics, bioinformatics, and machine learning to understand blood stem cell aging and develop targeted therapies for myeloid leukemias.

We also had fun in the evening with some Schnitzel (again).
